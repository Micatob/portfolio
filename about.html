We make tech work for real businesses

Micatech is a lean IT firm — not an agency, not a body shop. We solve the four problems every small business faces: security, connectivity, support and a web presence that actually converts.

Our Mission

Technology should help you serve customers, not create stress. At Micatech, we deliver enterprise thinking at SME scale: secure networks, monitored systems, responsive support and simple websites that are easy to own.

We started with a SOC mindset — assume breach, monitor everything, automate what’s boring — and applied it to the everyday IT that small brands actually need: reliable internet sharing, printers that work, backups that run, and a site that loads fast on a phone in Benin City or Lagos.

No lock-in, no jargon. Just clean builds, documented handover, and someone who answers when you call.
